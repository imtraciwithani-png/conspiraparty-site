Two quick table games

Cover story, 60 seconds

Point at a dish. That person has one minute to explain it as a cover story. Who made it, what it is hiding, and who benefits. A phone timer ends the speech. The table names the least believable cover story. That cook does not do the dishes.

One-sentence thanks

Go around once. Each thanks must include one real food from the table and one suspicious detail. “I am glad the rolls exist, and I think they arrived with a handler.” No second sentence. If someone starts a speech, the person on their left says “the pies need a witness” and the turn moves on.
