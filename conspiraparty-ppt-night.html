What is a PowerPoint party?

PowerPoint parties (also called PowerPoint Night) are a trend where each guest makes a short, silly slideshow and presents it to the group. Think “My cat is a government drone” with fake charts and dramatic pauses.

Ours is ready-made. You don’t ask guests to build slides. You present a classified 10-slide briefing, then they draw topic cards and get 60 seconds each. Same energy, way less prep.

How to host (step by step)

  1. Send the invite. Mark it TOP SECRET. Tell guests to come dressed as their favorite theory (or just wear foil). Print our 5×7 invite or text a photo of it.
  2. Open the slides. Google Slides or PowerPoint on a TV, laptop or tablet. Speaker notes have every host line.
  3. Present the briefing. Walk through five “theories” (birds, daylight saving, cats, the moon, missing socks). Laugh. Deny everything.
  4. Guest turn. Pass out topic cards. Each agent gets 60 seconds to pitch. No notes. No mercy.
  5. Vote and award. Ballot for Most Convincing and Tinfoil Hat of the Year. Hand out certificates. This briefing never happened.
