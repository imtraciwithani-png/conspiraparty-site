Believers say · Online movement since ~2017

QAnon

Believers say: anonymous "Q" drops hint at a secret war against a cabal. What's documented: an online phenomenon tracked by journalists and the HBO series Q: Into the Storm; posts appeared on imageboards; the movement spilled into real-world politics. We roast the certainty theater on every side of the comment section.

Glossary: Q drop Q: Into the Storm Weird News (QAnon tag) Vote this week

Believers say · 2016 viral theory

Pizzagate

Believers say: coded messages in leaked emails pointed to a DC pizza shop. What's documented: the shop was real; the coded-cabal claim was not proven; a man fired a rifle inside the restaurant in Dec 2016 while "investigating," was arrested, and later apologized — a real crime sparked by an online theory. Left and right media both turned it into a content machine. We file it under "don't bring a gun to a meme."

Believers say · Permanent bureaucracy lore

Deep State

Believers say (right-leaning version): career intel/bureaucracy sabotages populist presidents. Believers say (left-leaning version): corporate capture, dark money, and revolving-door lobbyists are the real shadow government. What's documented: large permanent agencies, classified programs, lobbying, and revolving doors all exist (Church Committee, Snowden leaks, FEC filings). The mystical all-knowing cabal is the party trick. Roast both speeches.

Glossary: Deep State Snowden / PRISM COINTELPRO

Believers say · 2016 and 2020

"Stolen election" claims (both cycles)

2016 believers (left-leaning): Russian interference and Comey's letter stole the race. What's documented: U.S. intel assessed Russian influence ops; Mueller investigated; Trump still won the Electoral College. 2020 believers (right-leaning): widespread fraud stole the race. What's documented: officials from both parties certified results; dozens of lawsuits failed; some people were charged over Jan 6. We roast the "my loss = conspiracy, your loss = democracy" hypocrisy on both teams.

Glossary Watergate (actual stolen-ops vibes) Weekly vote

Believers say vs court record

Epstein "list" lore

Believers say: a secret client list will take down everyone. What's documented: Jeffrey Epstein was a convicted sex offender; he died in federal custody in 2019; civil and criminal court filings, flight logs, and later unsealed documents name many associates — naming someone in a document is not the same as a proven crime by that person. We do not invent charges. Bring popcorn and primary PDFs, not pitchforks.

Court-record summary Glossary: client list

Believers say · 1963 onward

JFK assassination theories

Believers say: CIA, mob, Cubans, LBJ, or a combo — anything but a lone gunman. What's documented: Warren Commission concluded Oswald acted alone; House Select Committee later found a "probable conspiracy" on acoustic evidence that remains disputed; millions of pages released via the JFK Records Act. Still the champagne of corkboards.

JFK records note Starter books Stone faces elsewhere

Believers say · Sky lines

Chemtrails / weather control

Believers say: those lines are spraying you on purpose; weather is a weapon. What's documented: contrails are a known aviation physics thing; governments have researched weather modification (e.g., cloud seeding) in limited programs; a global secret spray-cabal remains unproven. Pair with the Chemtrail Cowboys tee and a meteorology textbook.

Believers say · Money & pharma lore

Soros / Big Pharma / "they"

Right-leaning believers say: George Soros puppet-masters protests and elections. Left-leaning believers say: Koch networks, pharma PACs, and billionaire donors buy policy. What's documented: billionaires on every team fund politics (FEC, OpenSecrets); drug companies lobby hard; scapegoating one surname while ignoring the rest is fan fiction. Follow the money — all of it.
