How to dig

  1. Follow the primary document — reports, FOIA/declassified files, court unsealings, hearing transcripts — before the hot take.
  2. Check the date — old files get remixed as breaking news. Timelines matter.
  3. Ask who benefits — from the claim, from the denial, and from your attention.
  4. Compare at least two sources — a Primary document plus an Independent or Believers voice, not just one feed.

Note: Political and fringe rabbit holes are for critical thinking and conversation. We label what believers say, what's on the record, and unanswered questions. No verdicts from us. No inventing claims about real people.
