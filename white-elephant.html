Three names, one swap

Everyone brings one wrapped gift. Players take turns. You may open a new gift or steal one that is already open. That is the whole plot.

  • White Elephant: the usual name. Funny, useful, or wonderfully odd gifts under a cap you announce first.
  • Dirty Santa: the same rules with a sillier nickname. On this page it still means kind jokes, not cruel ones.
  • Yankee Swap: the New England name for the same turn order and the same steal limit.

The rules, in order

  1. Bring one wrapped gift. Agree on a spending cap before anyone shops. Write it in the invite so nobody guesses.
  2. Draw numbers. The free PDF has tags 1 through 30. Cut them, shuffle, and hand them out.
  3. Number 1 opens a gift and shows the room.
  4. Every next player chooses: open a wrapped gift, or steal an opened one that is not frozen.
  5. If your gift is stolen, you go again right away. Open a new gift or steal a different one. You may not take back the gift that was just taken from you.
  6. Three steals, then freeze. After the third steal, that gift stays with that player for the rest of the game.
  7. The last number may steal any unfrozen gift or open the last wrapped one.
  8. Optional ending. Number 1 may keep their gift or make one swap with any gift that is not frozen.

Variations that still behave

  • Kid round: no steals. Open gifts in number order, then allow one polite trade if both kids agree.
  • Short game: freeze a gift after two steals when the group is under eight people.
  • Theme table: only mugs, only books, or only snacks. Announce the theme with the price cap.
  • Left-right story: skip it. The number draw is easier to explain to a mixed room.

Office and family tips

  • Say the cap, the freeze rule, and the “kind gifts only” rule before anyone shops.
  • At work, ban gifts that punch down, name a coworker, or need a long explanation from HR.
  • At home, let the youngest players draw the early numbers so they are not waiting through every steal.
  • Wrap everything the same way. Fancy paper is a tell.
  • Play while people are eating. A hungry steal is a louder steal.

FAQ

Is White Elephant the same as Dirty Santa and Yankee Swap?

Yes. Three names, one gift swap: open a present or steal one, and a gift freezes after three steals.

How many times can a gift be stolen?

Three times. After the third steal it freezes and stays with that player.

Can you steal a gift right back?

No. If yours is stolen, open a new gift or steal a different one.

Is this okay for kids and offices?

Yes. Skip steals for little kids, or allow one polite trade. At the office, set a price cap and keep gifts kind.
