Three names, one swap

Everyone brings one wrapped gift. Players take turns. You may open a new gift or steal one that is already open. That is the whole plot.

  • White Elephant: the usual name. Funny, useful, or wonderfully odd gifts under a cap you announce first.
  • Dirty Santa: the same rules with a sillier nickname. On this page it still means kind jokes, not cruel ones.
  • Yankee Swap: the New England name for the same turn order and the same steal limit.

The rules, in order

  1. Bring one wrapped gift. Agree on a spending cap before anyone shops. Write it in the invite so nobody guesses.
  2. Draw numbers. The free PDF has tags 1 through 30. Cut them, shuffle, and hand them out.
  3. Number 1 opens a gift and shows the room.
  4. Every next player chooses: open a wrapped gift, or steal an opened one that is not frozen.
  5. If your gift is stolen, you go again right away. Open a new gift or steal a different one. You may not take back the gift that was just taken from you.
  6. Three steals, then freeze. After the third steal, that gift stays with that player for the rest of the game.
  7. The last number may steal any unfrozen gift or open the last wrapped one.
  8. Optional ending. Number 1 may keep their gift or make one swap with any gift that is not frozen.

Game variations

  • Steal limits. Three steals is the standard freeze. Use two steals when the group is under eight people, or one steal when the office lunch is short. Say the number out loud before number 1 opens anything.
  • Dice. Skip the number draw. On your turn, roll one die and do what it says. The free sheet below is the whole rules page. Print it and put it in the middle of the table.
  • Left/right story. Read a short holiday story. Every time you say “left,” everyone passes their wrapped gift left. Every time you say “right,” they pass right. When the story ends, you open the gift in your hands. No steals. Good for a big circle that does not want to track a freeze count.
  • For adults. Keep the same turns. Change the gift brief: funny, useful, or oddly specific. Agree on a spending cap in the invite. Skip gifts that need a private explanation later.
  • Office. Same rules, kinder gifts. No gifts that name a coworker, punch down, or belong in an HR file. A shared cap and the freeze rule, written in the invite, prevent the surprise luxury gift.
  • Kid round. No steals. Open gifts in number order, then allow one polite trade if both kids agree.

White Elephant dice game

Everyone brings one wrapped gift and sits in a circle. Roll one die on your turn. The gift in front of you at the end is yours.

  1. 1 — Open. Open a wrapped gift from the center pile and keep it in front of you.
  2. 2 — Left. Steal the gift from the person on your left. They roll next, out of turn, unless the pile is empty and they have nothing to open.
  3. 3 — Right. Steal from the person on your right. Same follow-up roll.
  4. 4 — Pass left. Everyone passes the gift in front of them one seat to the left. Still-wrapped gifts in the center stay put.
  5. 5 — Pass right. Everyone passes one seat to the right.
  6. 6 — Swap. Trade with any player, or open a wrapped gift if you do not have one yet.

Freeze. A gift that has been stolen three times stays with that player. It cannot be stolen or passed. Mark it with a scrap of the wrapping paper.

End. Stop when every gift is open, or after each player has rolled three times, whichever comes first. No take-backs on the gift you just lost.

Office and family tips

  • Say the cap, the freeze rule, and the “kind gifts only” rule before anyone shops.
  • At work, ban gifts that punch down, name a coworker, or need a long explanation from HR.
  • At home, let the youngest players draw the early numbers so they are not waiting through every steal.
  • Wrap everything the same way. Fancy paper is a tell.
  • Play while people are eating. A hungry steal is a louder steal.

FAQ

Is White Elephant the same as Dirty Santa and Yankee Swap?

Yes. Three names, one gift swap: open a present or steal one, and a gift freezes after three steals.

How many times can a gift be stolen?

Three times. After the third steal it freezes and stays with that player.

Can you steal a gift right back?

No. If yours is stolen, open a new gift or steal a different one.

Is this okay for kids and offices?

Yes. Skip steals for little kids, or allow one polite trade. At the office, set a price cap and keep gifts kind.

How does the White Elephant dice game work?

Sit in a circle with one wrapped gift each. Roll one die: 1 open a gift, 2 steal left, 3 steal right, 4 everyone passes left, 5 everyone passes right, 6 trade with anyone. A gift freezes after three steals.

What is the left/right story version?

Read a short story. Pass gifts left when you hear “left” and right when you hear “right.” Open whatever you are holding when the story ends. There are no steals.
