Timeline Collapse: The Parade That Waited

Posted 11/27/2026 · ← All field reports

Full disclosure: this is commentary and jokes. Facts come from the linked reports, so click through and judge for yourself. The party kits linked here are our own products.

Happy day-after-Thanksgiving, agents. The pie is gone, the group chat is at war, and the internet spent the holiday finding conspiracies in places where there was just weather. Today’s file has a parade that got moved and broke reality, a meme that came back from 2024 for a second helping, a holiday message with a grudge list, and the underground society New York has whispered about for decades. Spoiler: it has folding chairs.

XFacebookPinterest

Exhibit A: The parade that waited

Per Newsweek, the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade got bumped to Saturday for the first time in its history. The forecast blew past the balloon safety rules, the city was under a severe weather emergency, and Central Park logged its wettest Thanksgiving since 1870. NBC aired last year’s parade instead. Within hours, per the New York Post, TikTok decided America had suffered a “timeline collapse,” Reddit brought up the Illuminati, and astrology fans blamed a supermoon in Gemini. Over at The Daily Beast, conservative commentators blamed Mayor Zohran Mamdani personally, with one saying the mayor “hates America.”

Let’s check the file. Newsweek notes the weather rules came from a task force led by former Mayor Rudy Giuliani. So the deep-state plot to cancel Snoopy was written by Rudy Giuliani in the early 2000s. That’s a long con. Also, a timeline collapse that only moves a parade two days is the laziest multiverse ever. If reality actually broke, we’d at least get a Snoopy who can talk. And yes, Mamdani fans, a mayor saying “safety first” on a day his city is underwater isn’t courage. It’s reading the weather app. Nobody gets a medal here.

Exhibit B: The trad wife rerun

Indy100 reports that JD Vance’s Thanksgiving 2024 post is making the rounds again. It’s the Norman Rockwell “Freedom From Want” parody with Vance’s face on the housewife, Trump standing behind him, and a mostly red electoral map where the turkey should be. After this month’s midterm losses, people online asked for an updated version.

The internet never forgets, and it really never forgets a man who chose to be the lady holding the turkey. In 2024 the platter was a landslide. In 2026 it’s leftovers. Fair warning to everyone posting victory memes this year, no matter which team you’re on: your Thanksgiving gloat has a two-year shelf life, and the internet keeps receipts in the freezer.

Exhibit C: The grievance roll call

Per USA Today, President Trump’s Thanksgiving post wished a happy holiday to, among others, “those who issued fake Economic Numbers,” after the Commerce Department revised third-quarter growth up from 3.4% to 4.3%, and he called for an investigation. Then he wished it to his critics, the White House renovation critics, and a long list of people he doesn’t like. Meanwhile, Kamala Harris posted a polished Thanksgiving Q&A video covering her signature dish, host gifts and the status of Doug Emhoff’s “onion goggles.”

Skeptic’s note: this might be the first conspiracy theory where the numbers got better and that’s the scandal. “They secretly made the economy look worse than it was” is a twist no one saw coming. On the other side of the table, an onion goggles update is the most focus-grouped thing that’s ever happened to a vegetable. One guy sends Happy Thanksgiving like it’s a court summons. The other team sends it like a brand partnership. Somebody just say “pass the rolls” like a normal person.

Exhibit D: The sewer gold rush

The New York Post reports, citing sources, that the so-called “mole people” seen climbing out of a Manhattan manhole last week are apparently panning for gold in the lower levels of the sewer, sometimes with tables and chairs, shaking metal in cups “like a bartender shaking a martini.” Gothamist says police are looking for the group, and no arrests had been made.

So the hidden underground society is real, and it’s a pop-up casino with folding chairs. Every lizard-people theorist in America just got their hopes up and dashed in one paragraph. They’re not reptiles. They’re prospectors. It’s 1849 down there, only wetter and with worse smells. Skeptic’s note: trespassing in a sewer is a terrible idea, but the “secret tunnels under New York” crowd has been waiting decades for this, and what they got was a dude with a cup.

The pattern on the corkboard

Today’s files all have the same twist: the boring answer was right there. The parade moved because of wind. The meme came back because the internet has a calendar. The economy “scandal” was good news. The secret underground society was a gold panning hobby with lawn furniture. The truth isn’t out there. It’s in the weather report, the archive and the sewer. Check your sources, then go eat a leftover pie.
