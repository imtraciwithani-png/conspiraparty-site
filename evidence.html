Party Evidence

Had a Conspiraparty night? Send a photo. Once a month, one submission gets a free digital kit. That is a kit we already sell, sent as a download. No cash, no new price.

How to enter

  1. You bought a kit, or you used the free printables at a real party.
  2. Send one to three photos of the party. Faces are fine if everyone in the photo is fine with it.
  3. Use the existing submit form, or email traciandersonart@gmail.com with the subject “Party evidence”.
  4. Tell us the first name we can print, and whether we may show the photo on this page later.

We pick one winner each month from what actually arrives. The prize is a free digital kit, sent by email. If a month is quiet, we wait.
