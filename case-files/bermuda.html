CASE 006

Bermuda Triangle

A busy patch of Atlantic ocean with a dramatic nickname. Weather and traffic do most of the paperwork.

Suspicion meter

33% — A nickname with a tide chart.

Evidence

  • Writer Vincent Gaddis popularized the name in a 1964 Argosy article, “The Deadly Bermuda Triangle.”
  • The U.S. Board of Geographic Names does not list the Bermuda Triangle as an official place name.
  • The area between Florida, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico is a real, busy shipping lane with hurricanes and the Gulf Stream.

The public record, lightly

  • Miami, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico are real. The triangle is a line people draw between them.
  • Coast Guard and insurers have long treated the region as ordinary ocean once you count the traffic.

How to use this at your party

Draw the triangle on a paper tablecloth. Guests mark where the dip went.
