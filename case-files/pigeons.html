CASE 005

The Pigeon Bureau

A satire, said out loud so nobody mistakes it for a briefing. The birds are real. The spy story is a joke.

Suspicion meter

12% — They work for crumbs, not a bureau.

Evidence

  • “Birds Aren’t Real” is a parody started by Peter McIndoe around 2017. He has said in public interviews that it is satire.
  • Rock pigeons are real city birds, descended from domesticated rock doves.
  • The joke works because it copies the shape of a cover story, then winks.

The public record, lightly

  • Columba livia is the ordinary city pigeon. Parks are full of them.
  • The satire is recent and openly labeled as satire by its creator.

How to use this at your party

Appoint a Pigeon Liaison. Their only power is announcing when the snacks are compromised.
