CASE 001

Bigfoot

A large, camera-shy walker. The name Bigfoot spread after a 1958 Humboldt Times story about oversized footprints at Bluff Creek, California.

Suspicion meter

62% — Footprints. No forwarding address.

Evidence

  • The 1958 Bluff Creek prints were reported in the Humboldt Times and picked up by newspapers across the country.
  • The Patterson-Gimlin film was shot at Bluff Creek on October 20, 1967. People still argue about the figure in the frames.
  • No museum holds a verified Bigfoot body. The file is footprints, stories, and blurry pictures.

The public record, lightly

  • Bluff Creek is a real creek in Northern California. The prints were a news story, not a lab result.
  • “Sasquatch” is the name used in the Pacific Northwest, from stories told long before the 1958 headline.

How to use this at your party

Put paper and a pan of mud by the door. Guests leave a print and invent the alibi.
