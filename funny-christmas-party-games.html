Christmas would you rather

Read one pair. The room votes by pointing. The loser of a tie has to hum one line of a carol. Stop after eight if the cookies are ready.

  • Explain the fruitcake’s origin story, or eat one slice with the lights on.
  • Wear the ugly sweater you own, or the one a coworker picks from the lost-and-found.
  • Carol one song at your boss’s door, or sit next to your boss for the whole party.
  • Be in charge of the playlist, or be in charge of the cheese plate.
  • Open a gift with no card, or give a gift with a card you have to read aloud.
  • Spend the party by the speaker, or spend it by the person telling the long story.
  • Take the last cookie and confess, or leave it and spend the night wondering.
  • Do the left/right gift story, or roll the White Elephant dice game instead.

Who ate Santa’s cookies?

A mini mystery for 4 to 12 people. Read the case. Give the room three minutes. Then read the host answer on the next printed page.

The scene. A plate of four cookies and a full glass of milk were on the hearth at 8:00. At 8:20 the cookies are gone. The milk is still full. Four suspects were in the room: the Elf on the Shelf, Comet the reindeer, the neighbor kid, and the family dog.

  1. A crumb trail runs from the plate to the dog bed, then stops. The dog bed is clean. The crumbs sit on top of the blanket, not pressed in.
  2. One red felt fiber is stuck in the icing. The elf’s suit is red felt. Comet’s harness is leather. The kid is in a green hoodie. The dog is short-haired and brown.
  3. The milk is untouched. The kid asked for milk earlier and drank a different glass. The dog’s bowl is empty, and the dog is still looking at it.
  4. The elf’s usual perch is empty. A tiny icing print, the size of a felt foot, is on the mantel where the elf sits.

How to play at a party. Read the clues once. People point at a suspect. The loudest wrong answer has to refill the cookie plate.

Host answer

Do not hand this block to the players. Fold it under, or print it on the back.

The elf did it. The crumb trail was placed on top of the blanket, so the dog did not walk it. The red fiber matches the suit, not the harness, the hoodie, or the dog. The milk was left because the elf does not drink from a full glass. The icing print on the mantel is the elf’s foot. Comet would have left a leather scuff and a lower print. The kid already had milk. The dog is still waiting on an empty bowl.
