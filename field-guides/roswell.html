ROSWELL, NM

1947 debris. 2026 gift shops. Same town. Different clearance levels.

Field rule: Crash-site access is a hike on BLM land near Corona — no vehicles past the trailhead parking. Respect private property. Downtown attractions are the easy mode.

Roswell turned a 1947 ranch-country debris story into a full municipal aesthetic: alien streetlamps, UFO museums and a Main Street that leans all the way in. The alleged crash site is actually closer to Corona, about 75 miles north on BLM land — hike only, no vehicles. Downtown is where the souvenir industrial complex thrives.

Suspicious Spots

Public landmarks only. Stay where guests are welcome.

International UFO Museum and Research Center

114 N Main St. The mothership of downtown lore — exhibits, archives and gift shop. Open daily.

Roswell Visitor Center

426 N Main St. Maps, tips and a free souvenir photo with the Visitor Center aliens.

Alien Zone: Area 51

216 N Main St. Cheesy photo sets. Lean in.

UFO Spacewalk / Spaceport Roswell area

E 2nd St blacklight and VR-ish experiences for when walking Main Street isn't weird enough.

Alleged UFO Skip Site (near Corona)

BLM parking off Lincoln County Rd B007 / Transwestern Road; ~1 mile hike. No vehicles on the trail. Far from downtown — plan fuel and water.

Agent Notes

  • The 1947 incident was handled through Roswell Army Air Field; the debris field most people mean is nearer Corona than the city limits.
  • Air Force reports later framed Project Mogul / balloon explanations. The town framed tourism. Both can be true in different ways.
  • Bottomless Lakes State Park and Bitter Lakes NWR are excellent non-alien side quests nearby.
  • Buy local. The alien economy funds real humans.
