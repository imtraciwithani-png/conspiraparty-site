STONEHENGE

Neolithic GPS. Tour buses. Still unexplained enough for a party.

Field rule: Stay on visitor paths. Do not touch or climb the stones. Special inner-circle access is ticketed and timed when offered — never hop barriers.

Stonehenge on Salisbury Plain, Wiltshire, is a UNESCO World Heritage site managed with a visitor centre, shuttle and a path around the stone circle. Most visitors don't walk among the stones (special access is limited). Ancient astronomy, engineering flex, or prehistoric party venue — your field notes, your call.

Agent Notes

  • Bluestones and sarsens were hauled ridiculous distances by Neolithic people with Neolithic tech. That's already weird enough.
  • English Heritage manages access; inner-circle visits are limited — book officially if that's your mission.
  • Replicas exist worldwide (including U.S. roadside versions). Collect them like Pokémon if you must.
  • No drones over the monument. No chisels. No "just one touch."
